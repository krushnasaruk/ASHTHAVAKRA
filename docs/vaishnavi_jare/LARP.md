### 1. 🤡 "Why didn't you just use an Apple Watch or a FitBit?"
* **What the judge thinks:** *"Smartwatches have sensors. Hands have sensors. Why build a glove?"*
* **The 5-Second Mic Drop:**  
  > *"Because an Apple Watch sits on your wrist. It literally cannot tell whether your index finger is flexed or extended."*
* **The Deeper Techno-Babble Bluff:**  
  > *"An Apple Watch measures whole-forearm gross acceleration and photoplethysmography (heart rate). Hand rehabilitation requires isolated distal phalanx excursion across each individual digitorum tendon. You cannot decode individual 5-finger kinematic flexion or isolated index tapping from an accelerometer strapped 5 centimeters above the carpal tunnel."*
* **The Golden Pivot:**  
  > *"Look at our live 2D hand visualization right here—as I curl just my pinky finger, only the pinky bar animates. An Apple Watch would show zero movement."*

Our flex sensors provide continuous 50Hz resistive strain telemetry regardless of occlusion, ambient light, or hand orientation."*
* **The Golden Pivot:**  
  > *"With our glove, a patient can exercise under a blanket or while resting their hand naturally on their lap without having to mount a tripod."*
